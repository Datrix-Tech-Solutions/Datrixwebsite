Accounting

Sophisticated & Real-Time!

Factura’s sophisticated procurement, fulfillment and inventory management software allows you to record, analyze and report on your inventory, helping you manage your stock position and cost. Enjoy full stock visibility in real-time too.

Sales & Purchase

Factura allows you to create sales automation workflows to proactively manage your sales.

Effectively drive sales performance

Sales staff can create quotes (which convert to sales orders and invoices), based on standard items and pricing in the system.

Track, Report & Visualise

It tracks operational and financial data—by the business driver—to give you superior reporting power and a complete view of your organization. Create any kind of report, dashboard, or visualization you want—with exactly the metrics that matter

Order & POS

Streamline and manage your order fulfillment and returns effectively by using Factura’s embedded business processes.

The perfect POS software solution

Keep track of inventory across multiple stores, seamless syncing of stock. Use automated reordering based on specified stock levels, and adjust reorder points and restock levels to make sure you never have too much or too little stock on your shelves.

Quick Inventory Lookups

Move stock between stores or your warehouse with stock transfers, and quickly perform inventory lookups across all your sales channels.

Business Intelligence & Productivity

Factura includes numerous productivity tools to let you run your business more effectively and efficiently – increasing your profitability.

Sophisticated and Customizable Dashboards

Get real-time visibility across the business including bank balances, sales, upcoming bills, profitability and KPIs with Factura’s business intelligence software.

Value-Added Insights

Understand true value-added insights and gain instant visibility to identify issues, trends, and opportunities and immediately drill down to the underlying transaction and take action.

Over 150+ reports with many customization options

Project Accounting

Start and finish projects on time, stay on top of project costs and revenue, and keep projects productive and profitable.

Multi Currency

Easy multi-currency accounting. Create invoices, reconcile accounts and get paid in any currency.

Approval Workflow

Simple, multi-level approvals for any transaction. Approval process can be triggered based on the value of the transaction.

Multi Companies / Group Consolidation

Factura makes it easy to manage the financials for multiple entities, whether your business structure is simple or complex, domestic or global.

Cost / Profit Centre

Dimensions can do many things for any organization running Factura. They allow a company to have a very simple chart of accounts, while maintaining the ability to report and analyze data on multiple different levels and criteria.

Bank Reconciliation

Easily reconcile your bank statement against your bank account register to keep your Factura account accurate.

Budgets with Variance Analysis
